SuperDoc ships with built-in UI for the toolbar, comments panel, and tracked-change review. When that UI fits, use it. When you need your own — a custom toolbar with your design system, a sidebar that lives where your product wants it, a review surface that matches your workflow — `superdoc/ui` gives you the state and actions to drive it.

## The layer model

Three layers, each with a clear job:

| Layer | What it is | When you reach for it |
|---|---|---|
| **Document API** (`editor.doc.*`) | Stateless request/response contract. Runs in the browser and on the server. | Read or mutate document content: comments, tracked changes, formatting, structure. |
| **`superdoc/ui`** | Browser-only UI controller. Subscriptions, viewport geometry, per-command state. | Bind your UI to live editor state. Drive your toolbar, sidebar, review panel. |
| **Built-in modules** | Optional UI SuperDoc renders for you (comments panel, toolbar, floating bubble). | You want SuperDoc's UI as-is. Skip this layer when you're building your own. |

The Document API is what mutates the document. `superdoc/ui` is what your UI subscribes to. Built-in modules are SuperDoc's own consumer of those layers — turn them off when you're providing your own.
